# Rituals & Reflection – Quick Start Guide

Aurora now supports daily focus rituals, weekly reflection, and smart nudges that adapt to your energy. This guide helps you activate the loop in under five minutes.

---

## 1. Set Your Rhythm

1. Open **Settings → Rituals & Nudges**
2. Choose your **Morning Ritual** and **Evening Reflection** times (default: 8 AM / 6 PM)
3. Pick a **Weekly Review** slot (default: Sunday @ 7 PM)
4. Optional: configure quiet hours, nudge intensity, and trigger categories

Aurora adapts to your schedule immediately—no restart required.

---

## 2. Morning Ritual

When the ritual window opens:

1. Review the **top three “Deserve Focus”** items (powered by CPS)
2. Tap **Commit to Focus** on the item you’ll tackle first
   - Aurora starts a focus session and boosts CPS momentum for 12 hours
3. Need help planning? Tap **“Break this into steps”** for an instant AI plan

Morning streaks grow with consecutive completions. Streak + completion rates surface in Insights.

---

## 3. Evening Reflection

During your chosen reflection window:

1. Log **Done / Deferred / Dropped** counts for the day
2. Capture a short reflection (“What moved? What resisted?”)
3. Tap **Generate Recap** for a one-sentence AI summary
4. Press **Log Reflection** to close the loop and reset for tomorrow

Evening reflections update Aurora’s momentum tracker and learning loops.

---

## 4. Weekly Review Ritual

Aurora guides you through five stages:

1. **Clear Inbox** – archive or convert stale items
2. **Review CPS Shifts** – see what rose in priority
3. **Distill Insights** – write or auto-generate takeaways
4. **Recommendations** – note what to share or adjust
5. **Next Week’s 3 Focuses** – set the anchors for morning rituals

Metrics (Focus Gravity, Capture vs Output, Clarity Index) update automatically after completion.

---

## 5. Smart Nudges

Aurora now delivers contextual nudges aligned with your energy:

- **Triggers:** stale high-priority items, capture slowdowns, extended fatigue, Express vs Distill imbalance, reflection reminders
- **Tone:** adapts to ARTE (Focused, Calm, Energized, Reflective, Fatigued)
- **Controls:** Settings → Rituals & Nudges (intensity, categories, quiet hours)
- **Throttling:** max 3 nudges/day and 1/hour; fatigue can suppress nudges automatically

Use the overlay buttons:
- **Let’s do it** – accepts and logs a positive response
- **Remind me later** – snoozes and holds off similar nudges
- **Dismiss** – tells Aurora to stand down

---

## 6. Insights Dashboard

- Visit **Insights → Overview** for the new **Focus Rituals** card
- Track completion rate, morning/evening streaks, last weekly review, and nudge response rate
- View a 14-day ritual consistency sparkline

---

## Tips

- Missed a ritual? Aurora reschedules the next window and resets streak gently
- Keep reflections short—consistency matters more than length
- Quiet hours silence nudges but rituals still appear in-app when opened
- Weekly review next focuses appear in morning ritual suggestions

---

**From resonance to reflection:** let Aurora coach your momentum and keep your focus orbit aligned.
