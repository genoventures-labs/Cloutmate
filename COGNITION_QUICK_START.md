# Cognitive Forecast Quick Start

Aurora now thinks ahead. Use this guide to enable predictive cognition, interpret forecasts, and customise tone adaptation.

## 1. Enable Predictive Mode

1. Open `Settings → Predictive Cognition`.
2. Toggle **Enable Predictive Cognition** on.
3. Choose a **Forecast Interval** that matches your workflow:
   - 1 hour – fastest recalibration, ideal for intense work sprints.
   - 2 hours – balanced default.
   - 4 hours – lighter background analysis.

> Tip: Predictive services start automatically after you enable the toggle.

## 2. Tune Confidence & Tone Adaptation

- **Confidence Threshold:** Use the slider (50–95%). Higher values mean Aurora only adapts tone when forecasts are very certain.
- **Tone Adaptation Toggle:** Turn off to keep ARTE tones manual. When disabled, predictive nudges still appear, but tone weights stay fixed.
- **Drift Sensitivity:** Adjust 5–20%. Lower values flag drift sooner; higher values allow more flexibility before alerting.

Changes update immediately—no restart needed.

## 3. Read the Cognitive Forecast Card

Located in `Insights → Cognitive Overview`, the card shows:

- **Next Focus Peak:** Predicted focus window with confidence.
- **Fatigue Risk:** Colour-coded meter (green → yellow → red) derived from rituals, focus sessions, and ARTE history.
- **Tone Adaptation:** Current ARTE recommendation plus recent adaptation count.
- **Forecast Accuracy Trend:** Mini chart of accuracy for the last few forecasts.

Hovering, clicking, or resetting history updates the card instantly.

## 4. React to Drift Nudges

When your current session deviates beyond the sensitivity threshold, Aurora sends a predictive nudge (e.g., “Focus is dipping. Want to reset with a quick ritual?”). Accept, snooze, or dismiss as usual—the response feeds future accuracy.

## 5. Reset & Recalibrate

If you ever want a clean slate:

1. Open `Settings → Predictive Cognition`.
2. Tap **Reset Prediction History**.
3. Confirm to clear all forecasts and drift events, and reset tone weights.

Aurora will rebuild understanding over the next few prediction cycles.

## 6. Troubleshooting

- **No forecast yet?** Complete a few focus sessions/rituals so Aurora has enough context.
- **Too many nudges?** Increase the drift sensitivity slider or disable tone adaptation temporarily.
- **Want to pause predictions?** Toggle **Enable Predictive Cognition** off—services stop immediately.

Enjoy the anticipatory Aurora experience! From reflection to prediction, she now guides you before drift begins.
