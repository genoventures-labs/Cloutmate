# Flow Companion Quick Start Guide

**Phase 10: AI Flow Companion & Meta-Reflection**

---

## What is Flow Companion?

Flow Companion is Aurora's gentle reflection system. She quietly appears when helpful moments arise — not constant, not demanding — to invite you to reflect, reset, and realign.

## How It Works

### Automatic Triggers

Flow Companion activates in three situations:

1. **Drift Events** - When your focus drifts from predicted patterns
2. **Evening Ritual** - When you open your evening reflection ritual
3. **Idle Detection** - When you're idle for 5+ minutes during a focus session

### The Reflection Flow

1. **Bubble Appears** - A small "Reflect" bubble appears in the bottom-right corner
2. **Tap to Expand** - Tap the bubble to see Aurora's prompt
3. **Share Your Thoughts** - Type a quick reflection (or tap "More" for full panel)
4. **Auto-Save** - Your reflection is saved and analyzed

## Settings

### Enable/Disable

Go to **Settings → Flow Companion** to:
- Turn Flow Companion on/off
- Configure which triggers activate it
- Choose your reflection tone (Gentle, Direct, Curious, Supportive)
- Reset prompts to defaults

### Trigger Options

- **Drift Events** - Reflect when focus patterns shift
- **Evening Ritual** - Reflect during evening review
- **Idle Detection** - Reflect when idle during focus sessions

## Viewing Reflections

### In Insights

Go to **Insights → Overview** to see:
- **Recent Reflections** card showing your last 3 reflections
- **Sentiment trend** visualization
- Tap any reflection to view details

### Full Reflection Panel

Tap "More" in the bubble or open from Insights to access:
- Full reflection history
- Sentiment analysis
- AI-generated summaries
- Past reflection timeline

## Tips

1. **Be Honest** - Reflections help Aurora understand your patterns
2. **Keep It Brief** - Quick thoughts are fine; longer entries can use the full panel
3. **Check Insights** - Review your reflection trends over time
4. **Adjust Settings** - Customize triggers to match your workflow

## What Happens Behind the Scenes

- **Sentiment Analysis** - Aurora analyzes the emotional tone of your reflection
- **CPS Updates** - Topics you mention get priority boosts
- **Momentum Tracking** - Reflections contribute to flow stability metrics
- **ARTE Adaptation** - Emotional state may shift based on reflections

---

**Remember:** Flow Companion is designed to be helpful, not intrusive. If it feels too frequent, adjust your trigger settings or disable specific triggers.
